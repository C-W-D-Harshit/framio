# Copy preferences

Product tone comes from BRIEF.md and DESIGN.md. Controls are concise; errors explain what
happened and what the user can do. User wording, required terminology, and accessibility matter.
Confirmed facts and assumptions stay distinct. Marketing copy has no invented capabilities,
testimonials, customer names, counts, measured results, prices, certifications, or offers.
Synthetic names, amounts, dates, and statuses are fine inside a coherent product demonstration.
They do not establish marketing proof. These preferences also cover imported component copy.
UI copy has no em dashes. Titles join the project name and Framio with a middle dot, such as `my-app · Framio`.
